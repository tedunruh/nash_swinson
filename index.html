About

2026 Shooting Stars, Arts Council of Johnson County

Hi! I’m Nash Swinson, a scenic designer and carpenter from the Kansas City area who has spent four-plus years in the shop across 24 productions, from the first pencil sketch to the last screw on load-in.

My work moves between design and build. One highlight is Almost, Maine, where as the scenic designer I drafted the ground plan by hand, built foam study models, and then led construction of the full-scale set. Another is Six, where I made hand-drawn drafting for the show’s towers to ensure they would be easy to assemble and disassemble for load-in and load-out when we took the show to the state conference.

One of my biggest accomplishments was being awarded 1st place in Johnson County’s Shooting Stars scholarship program for my design and production portfolio.

My most recent work was at Shawnee Mission Theatre in the Park in Kansas, where I worked as a seasonal scenic carpenter during the summer on three large-scale productions, gaining experience welding, driving and operating boom and scissor lifts, and reading and executing digital drafting.

I’m currently pursuing a BFA in Theatre Design Technology and Stage Management at Missouri State University, and I’m looking for seasonal summer stock work for the 2027 season.
